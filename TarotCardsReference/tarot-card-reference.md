# Complete Tarot Card Reference Guide

## Project Overview

This document provides comprehensive reference material for a complete 78-card tarot deck, including detailed descriptions, meanings, and interpretations for each card. This reference is designed for use in Product Requirements Documents (PRD) and Business Requirements Documents (BRD) for tarot-related applications, educational platforms, or divination tools.

## Major Arcana

The Major Arcana represents significant life events, spiritual lessons, and karmic influences. These 22 cards form "The Fool's Journey" - a narrative of spiritual evolution from innocence to enlightenment.

### 0. The Fool

**File Name:** `00-the-fool.png`

**Keywords:** New beginnings, innocence, spontaneity, free spirit

**Description:** A young traveler stands at the edge of a cliff, gazing upward with optimism. Carrying a small bundle and accompanied by a white dog, The Fool represents the beginning of a journey.

**Upright Meaning:**
- New adventures and opportunities
- Taking a leap of faith
- Embracing the unknown with optimism
- Living in the moment
- Innocence and pure potential

**Reversed Meaning:**
- Recklessness and poor judgment
- Holding back from opportunities
- Naivety leading to mistakes
- Fear of the unknown
- Foolish risks without consideration

**Symbolism:**
- White dog: Loyalty and protection
- Cliff edge: Risk and potential
- White rose: Purity
- Mountains: Challenges ahead
- Sun: Enlightenment and optimism

## Card Reading Guidelines

### Reading Positions

**Single Card Draw:**
- Used for quick insights or daily guidance
- Focus on the primary meaning and current situation

**Three-Card Spread:**
- Past - Present - Future
- Situation - Action - Outcome
- Mind - Body - Spirit

**Celtic Cross (10 Cards):**
1. Present Situation
2. Challenge/Obstacle
3. Distant Past
4. Recent Past
5. Possible Future
6. Immediate Future
7. Your Approach
8. Environment
9. Hopes and Fears
10. Outcome

### Interpretation Tips

**Consider Context:**
- Question being asked
- Position in spread
- Surrounding cards
- Querent's situation

**Reversed Cards:**
- Can indicate blockage, delay, or internalization
- May suggest the shadow aspect
- Sometimes indicates the opposite meaning
- Can mean "not yet" or "in excess"

**Card Combinations:**
- Multiple Major Arcana: Significant life events
- Multiple Court Cards: Many people involved
- Multiple same number: Theme is emphasized
- Suit patterns: Elemental influences

**Intuition:**
- Trust your intuitive impressions
- Note which symbols stand out
- Pay attention to colors and imagery
- Consider the querent's reaction
